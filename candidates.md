# One-line pitch candidates

Ten fresh one-sentence pitches, each at most 240 characters, aimed at a friend
who has never written code and is deciding whether to let an AI agent on
make.fun build their first app idea.

1. You already know what your app should do; tell an AI agent on make.fun in plain English, and by the time your coffee cools you'll have a first version to poke at and send back for changes.
2. Think of make.fun as a friend who can code: describe the app you've been imagining, watch an AI agent build a clickable first draft, then say "make this part simpler" and see it happen.
3. You don't need a bootcamp to test an app idea: hand it to an AI agent on make.fun, get something you can actually try, and steer the next version with the same words you used to describe it.
4. The fastest way to learn if your app idea works is to stop describing it and start using it: an AI agent on make.fun builds the first version from your words, and you decide what's next.
5. If you can text a friend what your app should do, you can get it built: an AI agent on make.fun turns that message into a real first draft you can try, tweak, or walk away from.
6. Give your idea to a make.fun AI agent the way you'd hand a painter a rough sketch: it puts a first version in your hands, you say what feels wrong, and it keeps improving it with you.
7. Your first app doesn't have to begin with a coding class; begin with a sentence to an AI agent on make.fun, and let it build a draft you can click through before you decide anything else.
8. Let an AI agent on make.fun do the typing while you do the thinking: describe your app idea once, get a working draft back, and change it whenever you like in ordinary words.
9. Nothing about your first app idea requires you to learn programming first: tell an AI agent on make.fun what you want to see, play with the version it builds, and keep the final call.
10. You keep the idea and the decisions; a make.fun AI agent just does the building, so your first app goes from "wouldn't it be nice" to something you can try in a single conversation.
