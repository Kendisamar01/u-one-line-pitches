# One-line pitch candidates

Each line is one sentence, at most 240 characters. Notes score persuasiveness,
clarity and truth for a friend who has never coded.

1. Before you spend a weekend learning to code, tell your app idea to an AI agent on make.fun and let it put a first version in front of you: a draft, not a promise, with you still in charge.
   - Persuasive: names the weekend of learning they can skip and caps the risk as a draft. Clear: plain words, one read. True: an agent can build a first version for review.

2. Tell an AI agent on make.fun the app you keep wishing existed; it builds a first draft you can try, and you keep the idea, the final say and the right to walk away.
   - Persuasive: the idea they already carry becomes the starting point, with an easy exit. Clear: short and conversational. True: a draft is a normal first result, not a finished product.

3. Hand your app idea to an AI agent on make.fun the way you'd hand a recipe to a friend who cooks: it does the building, you get a first version to try and the final say on every change.
   - Persuasive: familiar recipe image makes handing it over feel ordinary. Clear: the roles are obvious. True: the agent builds, the person decides.

4. If you can explain your app idea over coffee, you can have an AI agent on make.fun build its first version: no jargon, no homework, just a draft you can judge with your own eyes.
   - Persuasive: turns a skill they have into permission to start. Clear: very plain. True: describing an idea is enough to ask for a first version.

5. Let a make.fun agent turn your first app idea into something you can actually tap on, then tell it what to change; you bring the idea and the opinions, it brings the code, and nothing is set in stone.
   - Persuasive: makes the result tangible and reversible. Clear: the split of work is easy to grasp. True: iterating on a draft is what these agents do.

6. Your app idea doesn't need a coding class, it needs a first draft: describe it to an AI agent on make.fun, watch it build something you can try, then keep or change it with a plain sentence.
   - Persuasive: swaps an intimidating class for a small first step. Clear: direct. True: changes can be requested in ordinary language.

7. Think of a make.fun agent as a patient builder who works from your words: give it your first app idea, see a real draft, and keep the final say without learning the tools yourself.
   - Persuasive: patience removes the fear of asking naive questions. Clear: one image, no jargon. True: the agent works from a description.

8. You keep the idea, the choices and the right to walk away; a make.fun AI agent does the first build from your plain description, so a first app stops being a skill test and becomes a conversation.
   - Persuasive: control first, then a fresh turn of phrase. Clear: slightly longer but still one read. True: a first build is a conversation starter, not a promise.

9. Give an AI agent on make.fun the app idea you've been carrying around; it builds a first draft you can try, you say what to change, and you stay in charge without learning to code.
   - Persuasive: starts from something they already care about, then makes the next step tiny. Clear: everyday words. True: a draft and revisions are realistic.

10. You don't need to know how to code to get your first app built: tell an AI agent on make.fun what you have in mind, and it builds a first version you can try, question and change.
    - Persuasive: removes the entry barrier immediately. Clear: straightforward. True: the first version is for trying and improving, not for instant perfection.
